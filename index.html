Carys Richards

I bring passion and enthusiasm to coding. Combining my professional experiences in communications and game design, I approach projects with a producer mindset to deliver engaging, interactive experiences.

Known for my creative problem-solving and attention to detail, some of my key achievements include contributing to the branching narrative campaign for the Mass Effect board game, supporting game development on Dragon Age: The Veilguard, and solo-developing my own adventure platformer, That Cat Game, in Unreal Engine 5.

I have brought projects to life, from conception to completion, and am driven by learning new skills, overcoming technical challenges, and infusing joy and energy into everything I create.
