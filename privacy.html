Privacy Policy.

None of the apps covered by this policy collect, store, or share any personal data. They contain no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no user accounts. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

Who this policy covers

Carysaurus is the name under which Carys Richards publishes independent apps and games. This policy applies to the following apps, on every platform where they are distributed:

  • KittyPop!
  • Nimbus
  • Close Enough

If an app is not named above, this policy does not describe it.

What we do not do

Across every app listed above, we do not:

  • collect, request, or store personal information of any kind;
  • ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address;
  • use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services;
  • show advertising, or work with advertising networks;
  • use cookies or any similar technology for tracking or profiling;
  • build a profile of you, or track you across apps, websites, or devices;
  • share, sell, rent, or transfer data to third parties — there is no data to share.

The apps


KittyPop!

KittyPop! is an entirely offline game. It requests no Android permissions whatsoever, and has no network access — every image, sound, and piece of game data is bundled inside the app itself. Nothing is transmitted from your device, because the app has no ability to transmit anything.

The game saves your best score for each level on your own device, using your browser or device's standard local storage. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is not linked to you or to any identifier. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage through your device settings, removes it permanently.
