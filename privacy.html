Privacy Policy.

For apps published by Carysaurus. Last updated 16 August 2026.

Across every app listed above, we do not:

  • use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services;
  • show advertising, or work with advertising networks;

The one thing any app transmits is the coordinates Nimbus needs to fetch a forecast, which are sent to the weather services described below and to nowhere else. Nothing identifying you accompanies them.

The apps


KittyPop!

KittyPop! is an entirely offline game. It requests no Android permissions whatsoever, and has no network access — every image, sound, and piece of game data is bundled inside the app itself. Nothing is transmitted from your device, because the app has no ability to transmit anything.

The game saves your best score for each level on your own device, using your browser or device's standard local storage. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is not linked to you or to any identifier. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage through your device settings, removes it permanently.


Nimbus

Nimbus is a weather app. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising — but unlike KittyPop!, it does need the internet, because a forecast has to come from somewhere.

To show you a forecast, Nimbus needs to know which place you want the forecast for. You can either type a place name into the search box, or tap the location button to use your device's location. Your device's location is only ever read at the moment you tap that button — Nimbus never asks for it in the background, and never on start-up.

Whichever way you choose, the coordinates for that place are sent to two services in order to produce what you see on screen:

  • Open-Meteo, which returns the weather forecast for those coordinates.
  • BigDataCloud, which turns coordinates into a place name so the app can show you where you're looking at.

Nothing is sent with those requests except the coordinates themselves. No name, no account, no device identifier, no advertising ID — there is nothing attached that could identify you, and the requests are not linked to each other or to any profile. Carysaurus operates no server of its own, receives none of this information, and never sees that you used the app at all.

The most recently viewed location and your display preferences are saved on your own device, using your device's standard local storage, so the app can reopen where you left off. That information never leaves your device and is erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
